Who processes what

  • Local use (default). DeGoogle Kit runs on your Windows PC. Plan, checklist, scan results, Takeout files you choose, API keys, and trial/Pro status stay in AppData\DeGoogleKit. Opolyonix Corp does not receive that copy unless you later choose an optional cloud feature.
  • Optional free account. If you create an email/password account, Opolyonix Corp is the controller of that account. Processor: Supabase (Auth + Postgres), not Google. We store your email, a hashed password (Supabase Auth), your plan, and your guide checklist so they can follow you to another PC. We do not upload API keys, Takeout files, scan results, or clipboard contents.
  • Payments. Stripe processes Checkout for Lifetime Pro / Family. We do not store card numbers. After payment a signed license key can be linked to your account.
  • Updates and this website. GitHub (Microsoft) hosts the Windows download, version feed, and these pages. Checking for updates is optional and sends the app version in the User-Agent.
  • Optional Cloud AI. Only if you tick a separate consent box and use your own API key. The provider you pick (never Google Gemini) receives your question and a local scan summary. You can withdraw that consent in the app.

Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Provide the local app you asked to run — Art. 6(1)(b) contract / steps at your request.
  • Optional account (email, plan, checklist backup) — Art. 6(1)(a) consent, given when you create or sign in. Withdraw by deleting the account; local files remain until you erase them.
  • Paid license delivery and fraud prevention — Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f).
  • Optional Cloud AI — Art. 6(1)(a) consent, separate from the account.
  • Legal obligations (tax/accounting for a paid license) — Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not sell personal data, run ads, or use Google APIs. No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22).

Children: this app is not directed at children under 16. Use needs 16+ or a parent/guardian (GDPR Art. 8 default age).

If a processor stores data outside the EEA, Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision apply under that processor’s DPA. Stripe and GitHub may process in the US with appropriate safeguards.

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. In the app: Privacy → Export my data, Delete all local data, and Delete my cloud account. Withdraw account consent by deleting the account, and Cloud AI consent on the Coach tab.
